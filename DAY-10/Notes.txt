Animations in CSS:
    Properties:
        - animation-name
        - animation-duration
        - animation-timing-function
        - animation-delay
        - animation-iteration-count
        - animation-direction
    shorthand: myName 2s linear infinite normal

Media Queries: Help to create a Responsive website.

Positions:
- static: default position (top, right, bottom, left, z-index properties have No Effect)
- relative: element is relative to itself. (top, right, bottom, left, z-index will work)
- absolute: positioned relative to its Closest positioned ancestor (removed form the flow)
- fixed: positioned to relativeto browser.
- sticky: positioned to the user's scroll position.
z-index: It decides the "Stack level" of the elements.
z-index: auto(0)/ 1/ 2/ -1/ -2, ...
